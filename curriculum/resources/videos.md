# 🎥 Video Resource Library — 3D Printing Training

> A curated, categorized collection of YouTube tutorials and video resources covering every topic in the training curriculum. All videos are beginner-friendly unless marked **(Intermediate)** or **(Advanced)**.

---

## How to Use This Resource

- Watch videos in the order they appear within each section — they build on each other.
- Do not just watch — pause and replicate the steps on your machine.
- Mark videos complete in your training checklist.
- If a link is broken, search the channel name + video title on YouTube — channels frequently re-upload.
