## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Identify and explain key electronic components on a 3D printer
- Describe PSU ratings, safety, and voltage selection
- Explain how stepper motors and drivers work
- Read a wiring diagram and wire connectors safely
- Understand the MKS Eagle V1.0 (Snowflake) and Manta M8P V2.0 (Dragon/Twin Dragon) boards
