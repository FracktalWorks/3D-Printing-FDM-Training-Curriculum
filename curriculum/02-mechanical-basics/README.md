## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Compare Cartesian, CoreXY, and Delta kinematics
- Explain belts, lead screws, and linear rails
- Describe hot end assembly and filament melt zone
- Differentiate Bowden and direct-drive extruders
- Identify common mechanical failure modes
